Privacy

This site collects no personal data.

On this site

  • No account, no forms.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising or embedded social media.
  • Nothing is loaded from another site: everything is served from this domain.
  • The “Share” button uses your device's own share feature; nothing is sent to MESHORA.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare processes technical data (including IP addresses) to deliver pages and protect the service. MESHORA has no access to it to track visitors.
