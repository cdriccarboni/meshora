A network that connects the others.

Your messages, through every path available.

MESHORA connects your devices to each other and to the internet, so your messages find a way: the network when it's there, nearby devices when it isn't. Free, encrypted, with no central account.

In one minute

1. Open

Install the Android test version or open the web app. Your identity stays on your device: there is no account to create.

2. Connect

MESHORA checks the available, allowed paths: the internet first, then nearby devices when they are actually available.

3. Talk

Invite someone with a link or QR code. A message is only marked “delivered” after its signed receipt.

The labels are precise: “Internet available” means the relay is really responding. “Protected connection” refers to MESHORA's encrypted channel; it does not mean the whole phone is on a VPN. “Local mesh” will only be shown after a successful hardware test.

No gateway or relay can create a connection that isn't there: MESHORA makes the best of the ones that are.

Continuity

If the recipient can't be reached, the message stays on your device, marked “Waiting”, then leaves on its own as soon as a path exists. It is marked “Delivered” only once the recipient has acknowledged it with their key. Going through nearby devices, without the internet, is being tested.

Private, with no central account

Private conversations are end-to-end encrypted: the relay passes on messages it cannot read. No account, no ads, no trackers. Verifying contacts with a QR code is planned.

The web and networks you choose

Quick access

A simple space is planned to find your preferred web services and see whether they are accessible, filtered or offline. MESHORA will not ask for their passwords.

Protected connection

An optional VPN for an identified gateway is under study. It will never turn itself on, and no unlimited free VPN is promised.

Stay clear-eyed

A voluntary relay always needs its owner's consent. MESHORA cannot create internet access or guarantee access to an unavailable service.

Today, encrypted messaging over the internet is the verified path. Web access, social shortcuts and VPN are V1 work: they will only be announced with their limits and tests.

Open to the networks you already use

MESHORA doesn't replace your networks: it connects them. Under study for later: Nostr, publishing to Mastodon and Bluesky, and Meshtastic and MeshCore long-range radios through an external device.

Good to know

MESHORA is in testing. Do not use it in an emergency: call 112.

On iPhone, Apple strongly limits Bluetooth in the background. The iPhone app is planned; it will relay reliably while open and in a more limited way in the background. We'd rather tell you plainly.

No range or outage resilience is promised before it has been measured in the field.
