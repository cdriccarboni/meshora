Four pillars

MESHORA Mesh

Talk to nearby devices over Bluetooth or local network, without the internet.

MESHORA Connect

Use the internet connections and gateways that are actually available.

MESHORA Shield

Protect your privacy: encryption, and an optional VPN to your own gateway.

MESHORA Resilience

Stay in touch: messages kept while offline, then synced.

No gateway or VPN can create a connection that isn't there: MESHORA makes the best of the ones that are.
