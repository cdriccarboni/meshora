Install MESHORA

Two ways to use MESHORA: the web app, to try the interface right away, and the native apps, which are the only ones able to use Bluetooth and nearby networks.

Web app

It opens in your browser and can be added to your home screen.

Native apps

Links only appear here once each app has actually been published and tested on real devices.

What you'll need

A phone or a computer

No account to create. The app generates your identity on your device.

Bluetooth turned on

To exchange with nearby devices when the internet isn't there (native apps).

Optional: a gateway

A small computer at home (Raspberry Pi) to link your local network to the internet and protect your browsing.

Why the web app isn't enough

Browsers, especially on iPhone, don't let a web page use Bluetooth to link devices together. Features that work without the internet will therefore come with the native apps.
