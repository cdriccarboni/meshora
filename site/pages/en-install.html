Install MESHORA

MESHORA is in testing. Here is, for each device, what really exists today, how to install it and what you should know.

Android

Test version 0.2.1-beta Verified over the internet

End-to-end encrypted messaging through an internet test relay, delivery receipts, deferred sending after an outage. Tested on 26 October 2026 on a Google Pixel 9 with a computer, over Wi-Fi and 5G.

On a computer: shasum -a 256 MESHORA-0.2.1-beta-test.apk. The certificate can be read with apksigner verify --print-certs.

Install in 3 steps

  1. On the phone, tap “Download the APK”, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap “Install”.
  3. Open MESHORA. To exchange messages, go to the Network tab and enter the relay address and room given by the person who invited you.

This version is not on Google Play yet. Android or Play Protect may show a warning: only install MESHORA from this page or the project's GitHub page, and compare the fingerprint above.

The test relay is temporary and may be unavailable. Do not use it in an emergency: call 112.

Bluetooth without the internet In development

A test version for testers (0.3.0-alpha) links phones over Bluetooth, without the internet. It has not yet been verified on real devices: it is meant for people who want to help us test it.

See the Bluetooth test version and its instructions

Web app

It opens in the browser, on computers and phones, and can be added to your home screen.

To exchange with another device during testing, open the invitation link you were sent: it contains the test relay address, which the app asks you to accept.

iPhone and iPad

App planned — no version to install yet.

Meanwhile, the web app works in Safari to exchange over the internet with an invitation link. Bluetooth isn't available to web pages on iPhone: it will come with the app.

All platforms

What you'll need

A phone or a computer

No account to create: the app generates your identity on your device.

An invitation link

During testing, it gives the internet relay address and the room to join.

Bluetooth turned on

Only for the Bluetooth test version, on an Android phone or a computer with Chrome or Edge.
