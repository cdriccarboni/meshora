Install MESHORA

MESHORA is in testing. Choose your device, follow three steps, then read the real connection status: no account, no ads, no hidden promise.

Before you start

Android

The test APK lets you talk over the internet with an invited contact. Bluetooth remains experimental.

Web

Open the app in your browser for an online test. It does not replace the Android app for Bluetooth.

iPhone and iPad

The web version works over the internet. The native app and iPhone Bluetooth are not available yet.

After opening, rely on the status shown: the internet is available only when the relay responds. “Protected connection” describes encrypted MESHORA exchanges; a phone-wide VPN is not offered yet.

Install in 3 steps

  1. On the phone, tap “Download the APK”, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap “Install”.
  3. Open MESHORA: it connects by itself. To message someone, go to Contacts → Invite someone: they scan the QR code or open the link.

This version is not on Google Play yet. Android or Play Protect may show a warning: only install MESHORA from this page or the project's GitHub page, and compare the fingerprint above.

The MESHORA relay is in pre-production, with no availability guarantee. It never sees the text of your messages. Do not rely on MESHORA in an emergency: call 112.

Bluetooth without the internet In development

Version 0.3.0-beta2 includes an experimental Bluetooth link between phones, off by default (Network tab). It has not yet been verified between two real phones: it is for people who want to help us test it.

Outside your group, other devices only see encrypted data: neither your name nor your key.

Web app

To try MESHORA online with no installation, open the app in your browser, on a computer or phone. You can then add it to your home screen.

It connects automatically to the MESHORA relay. To exchange with someone, open the invitation link they sent you, or send yours from Settings → Invite someone.

iPhone and iPad

App planned — no version to install yet.

Meanwhile, the web app works in Safari to exchange over the internet with an invitation link. Bluetooth isn't available to web pages on iPhone: it will come with the app.
