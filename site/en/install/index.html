What you'll need

A phone or a computer

No account to create. The app generates your identity on your device.

Bluetooth turned on

To exchange with nearby devices when the internet isn't there.

Optional: a gateway

A small computer at home (Raspberry Pi) to link your local network to the internet and protect your browsing.

Follow progress

Code and documentation are published as soon as they're ready. The progress tracker shows what actually works today.
