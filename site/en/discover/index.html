No gateway or VPN can create a connection that isn't there: MESHORA makes the best of the ones that are.

Continuity

When the internet slows down or drops, MESHORA carries your messages through nearby devices, then syncs them as soon as the connection is back. Nothing to switch on, and nothing is ever lost without you knowing.

Private, with no central account

Private conversations are end-to-end encrypted. No server account, no ads, no browsing logs. You can verify your contacts by scanning their code.

Open to the networks you already use

MESHORA doesn't replace your networks: it connects them. It's designed to work with Nostr and, later, to publish to Mastodon and Bluesky or relay through Meshtastic and MeshCore long-range radios.

Good to know

On iPhone, MESHORA relays messages reliably while the app is open, and in a more limited way in the background. That's an Apple rule, which we follow and would rather tell you about plainly.
